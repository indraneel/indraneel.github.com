Sustainable Jersey hackathon

read the story | see the slides | github

2017

Technologies: React, Redux

Won 2nd place in a civic hackathon held by Sustainable Jersey in 2017. We spoke with a NJ town who wanted a solution to track their sustainability goals. We met with their green team leader a few times, and determined that visualizing their progress was important for volunteers to feel bought into the work. We also determined that the ability to share on social media.

Over the period of a week, I wrote a web dashboard app in React, featuring a Pinterest-style card layout for easy sharing. Good first foray into civic tech.
